    What is Just Click?

    Just Click is a free online click counter and click speed game. Every click adds one to your counter, fast clicks build a combo, and the game tracks your clicks per second (CPS) so you can try to beat your record. Your progress is saved in your browser, so you can come back any time.

    Just Click 是一個免費的線上點擊計數小遊戲：連續快速點擊可以累積連擊，還能測你的每秒點擊數（CPS）並解鎖成就。
